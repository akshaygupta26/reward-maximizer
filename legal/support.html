Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sync my offers?

Click the Reward Maximizer icon in your browser toolbar to open the popup. Then click a card button (e.g., "Amex" or "Chase") to open your card portal. Once you're on the offers page, the extension automatically syncs your offers.

Why don't I see a banner on a merchant website?

The merchant banner only appears if you have synced offers that match the website you're visiting. Make sure you've synced your offers first by visiting your card portal pages.

Is my data safe?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage. Nothing is sent to external servers. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I clear my data?

Open Settings (click the gear icon in the popup), scroll to "Data Management", and click "Clear All Data". This removes all synced offers and resets the extension.

Which cards are supported?

Currently: American Express and Chase, plus Rakuten for cashback stacking. Support for Citi, Capital One, Discover, Bank of America, and US Bank is coming soon.

How do I report a bug?

Email us at rewardmaximizer@gmail.com with a description of the issue, your Chrome version, and any error messages you see.
